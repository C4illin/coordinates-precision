How Geodesic Precision Works

Coordinates represent an uncertainty region on Earth's surface:

  • Rounding vs. Marker: Entering 35.6 with rounding defines the interval [35.55, 35.65]. The point marker sits at the exact nominal coordinate 35.60000… in the center.
  • Parallels of Latitude curve: Every line of constant latitude is a small circle around Earth's axis. On this 3D globe, the north and south edges visibly curve across the sphere.
  • Longitudinal Convergence: The width of 1° of longitude shrinks by cos(latitude). At 60° latitude, longitude is half the width of latitude.
